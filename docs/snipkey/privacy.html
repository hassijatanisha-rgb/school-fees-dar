Snipkey Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Snipkey – Text Expander ("Snipkey") is a browser extension that replaces short shortcuts you type with longer text you have saved.

What Snipkey stores

  • Your snippets and settings are stored in your own browser, using Chrome's extension storage. If you are a Pro user and turn on sync, they are also stored in Chrome's own sync storage so they appear in your other Chrome browsers signed in to the same Google account. That sync is provided by Google Chrome; we never receive the data.
  • Your Pro licence key (if you buy Pro) is stored in Chrome's extension storage.

What Snipkey does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share any personal data, browsing history, page content or anything you type.
  • It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no servers of its own.
  • It does not read or act in password fields.
  • It does not load remote code.

The content script runs on web pages only to notice when you type one of your shortcuts. It compares the few characters before your cursor with your own shortcut list, inside your browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.

The optional {clipboard} placeholder reads your clipboard only at the moment you expand a snippet that contains it, inserts the text, and keeps no copy.

Licence check (Pro only)

When you activate Pro, and about once a week after that, Snipkey sends your licence key and an installation ID to Lemon Squeezy (https://www.lemonsqueezy.com), our payment provider, to confirm the licence is valid. No snippet text or browsing data is included. Purchases themselves are handled by Lemon Squeezy under its own privacy policy.

Removing your data

Uninstalling Snipkey deletes the data it stored in your browser. Data you chose to sync is held by Chrome sync and can be removed by clearing your Chrome sync data in your Google account. You can also delete individual snippets in Snipkey's settings at any time. You can deactivate your licence in Snipkey's settings at any time.
