How subscriptions work

Pick a coffee and a rhythm. Skip, swap or pause from your account any time.

  1. Choose your coffee. A single origin, our house blend, or the roaster's pick.
  2. Set your schedule. Every week, two weeks or month, on the day that suits you.
  3. We roast to order. Your beans are roasted Monday and packed the same afternoon.
  4. Brew and tell us. Rate each bag and we'll tune your next pick.

Questions

When do you roast and ship?

We roast every Monday and Tuesday and ship the same day. Most orders arrive within 2–4 business days.

Whole bean or ground?

Whole bean keeps longest. If you choose ground, tell us your brewer and we'll set the grind to match.

Can I change my coffee?

Yes, swap your next bag up to the Sunday before roast day from your account page.

Are the bags recyclable?

Our bags are certified home-compostable, including the valve. The shipping box is 100% recycled card.
