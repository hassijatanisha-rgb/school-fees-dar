# Stitchly – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Stitchly is a browser extension that captures web pages as images. It is built to keep your data on your device.

**What Stitchly does with page content.** When you click the Stitchly icon (or use its shortcut or menu), Stitchly takes screenshots of the tab you are on and stitches them together in your browser. The images are stored locally in your browser (the 3 most recent captures, so you can reopen them) and are never uploaded anywhere. Stitchly cannot see other tabs, and does nothing until you start a capture.

**What leaves your device.** Only the licence check. If you buy Stitchly Pro and enter a licence key, the key and a random installation ID are sent to our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com), to activate the key, to re-check it about once a week, and to deactivate it if you choose. Your licence status is saved in Chrome's extension storage, which Chrome may sync between your own signed-in browsers.

**What Stitchly does not do.** No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no account, no cookies, no sale or sharing of data. Stitchly does not collect your browsing history or the contents of the pages you capture.

**Purchases.** Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record; their privacy policy covers the details you give at checkout (such as your email address for the receipt and licence key).

**Deleting your data.** Removing the extension deletes all stored captures and licence information from your browser. You can also deactivate your licence on the options page.

**Contact.** Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/hassijatanisha-rgb/school-fees-dar/issues or use the Support tab on the Chrome Web Store listing.
